HOW IT ALL BEGAN

Our Love Story

How we met

Our story began on the last night of the Araw ng Bayugan 3 celebration in 2024. Amid the lights, music, and excitement of the disco party, two strangers crossed paths without knowing that one simple encounter would change both of our lives forever. What started as a casual meeting quickly grew into meaningful conversations, genuine laughter, and a connection that felt effortless. That unforgettable night became the beginning of our beautiful love story.

Our Love Story

On June 15, 2024, we officially became a couple. Not long after, our love was tested by distance as we embraced a long-distance relationship. Though miles often separated us, our hearts remained close. Through countless video calls, heartfelt messages, unwavering trust, and constant prayers, we learned that true love isn’t measured by distance, but by commitment. Every challenge strengthened our bond and reminded us that the best things in life are always worth waiting for. Today, we stand hand in hand, ready to say “I do,” promising to choose each other every single day. This is not the end of our story—it’s only the beginning of our forever.

The Proposal

On January 2, 2026, while celebrating the extended celebration of New Year. We had no idea that one of the most unforgettable moments of our life was about to happen. As the rain gently fell from the sky, the heartfelt proposal happen. Standing there in the rain, surrounded by the people we love, the question that changed our lives forever asked . It wasn’t a perfect sunny day but it was perfectly ours. The rain made that moment even more magical, reminding us that love shines brightest even through life’s storms.
